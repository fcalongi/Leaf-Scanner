A leaf can span several hues — add one color per shade (dark veins, pale patches, reddish edges). Switch to the Exclude tab to punch out a color that's sneaking into the match. Add color finds a piece's outline for you: tap once inside it and it reads the color range up to where the photo actually changes color. Brush select paints exact pixels in by hand, for a spot no color match can reach. Lasso select traces a whole outline at once and fills in everything inside it.

Leaf Scanner runs entirely in this browser tab — your photos are never uploaded anywhere. Area is estimated as (leaf pixel count) × (calibration area ÷ calibration pixel count), the same core method described in Easlon & Bloom, Applications in Plant Sciences 2014, generalized here to any calibration color and tuned by hand or by auto-suggestion instead of a fixed Arabidopsis formula. For best accuracy, keep the leaf and calibration swatch in the same plane, avoid harsh shadows, and prefer a flatbed scan or a straight-down shot over a wide-angle close-up.
